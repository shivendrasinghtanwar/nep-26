# Contingency plans

> Pre-decide responses to the high-impact, medium-likelihood things that can go wrong. Re-decide nothing under stress.

## 1. Beni–Jomsom road closed (landslide / weather)

**Trigger:** Pokhara hotel + local enquiry on 2026-05-13 evening confirms the road is impassable for next 24 h.
**Decision:** [DRIVE / HOLD 1 day / SKIP MUSTANG]
**If skip:** add 14, 15, 16, 17 May to Pokhara workation + day trip to Bandipur. Surrender unused ACAP cost (non-refundable).
**If hold 1 day:** push everything 24 h, exit Nepal 24 May instead of 23 May → still inside permit buffer? *(verify against Bhansar end-date)*

## 2. Vehicle breakdown — India side

**Where most likely:** Yamuna Expressway midnight stretch, or Purvanchal toll plaza zone.
**Action plan:**
1. Call insurer roadside assistance: __________
2. Call Mahindra on-call (24×7): __________
3. Tow to nearest authorised Mahindra service centre.
4. If repair < 24 h: continue. If > 24 h: stay in nearest tier-2 city, push trip dates, evaluate flying back from Lucknow if cascading delay.

## 3. Vehicle breakdown — Nepal side

**Where most likely:** Beni → Jomsom gravel, Tatopani–Ghasa landslide zone, Muktinath switchbacks.
**Action plan:**
1. Stay with the vehicle. Hazard lights ON. Reflective triangle 50 m behind.
2. Call Nepal Police: 100. Call hotel (Pokhara base) for local mechanic referral.
3. If unrepairable in Mustang: arrange tow to Pokhara via local SUV operator. Estimated cost: _______ NPR.
4. Worst case: leave vehicle at Pokhara workshop, complete trip on hired SUV, return to collect.

## 4. Medical — altitude sickness

**Trigger:** persistent headache + nausea + ataxia (any two) at Jomsom or above.
**Action:** descend immediately to Beni or lower. Do not push to Muktinath.
**Backup:** Manipal Teaching Hospital, Pokhara — large, English-speaking. CIWEC clinics for travel medicine.

## 5. Medical — wife / driver illness

**Trigger:** any 24-h symptom that prevents driving.
**Action:** halt at nearest city. Wife is backup driver only for low-risk segments — never Mustang. If Shive is incapacitated mid-Mustang: call hotel for local driver pickup.

## 6. Network outage during a work commitment

**Trigger:** committed client meeting or deliverable falls during a no-coverage window.
**Mitigation upstream:** by 2026-05-07, pre-schedule any deliverable that falls 14–17 May or 21–22 May.
**Mitigation in-flight:** day-before backup — push full repo + work-in-progress to cloud + SSD before any Mustang leg.

## 7. Border issues at Sunauli (going in)

**Trigger:** Bhansar / Yatayat counter rejects authorization letter, or assesses commercial vehicle slabs.
**Escalation order:**
1. Show firm registration + GST + driving licence + e-RC stamped copy.
2. Politely request senior officer.
3. If still stuck: re-enter India side, return next day with a fresh letter — don't argue indefinitely.
4. Keep all transactions on receipt.

## 8. Border issues at Sunauli (going out)

**Trigger:** missing exit stamp on Bhansar / Yatayat = not closing the day-count for 2026 cap.
**Action:** insist on physical exit stamp. Photograph the stamped permits before crossing back into India. If you suspect a stamp was missed, return next morning to fix.

## 9. INR 2000 note slippage

**Trigger:** discover INR 2000 note in wallet after crossing into Nepal.
**Action:** do not try to spend it; carry to RBI exchange counter on return. Some Nepal authorised exchanges may take it but at deep discount.
