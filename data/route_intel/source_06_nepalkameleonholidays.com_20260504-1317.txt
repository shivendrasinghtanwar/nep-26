Fetched: 2026-05-04

KEY EXTRACT:
"From Beni it's about a 5-hour drive to Jomsom via Tatopani, Ghasa, Lete,
Kobang and Marpha. It's all the way rough road / off-road."

"Muktinath Road in April–May–June becomes fantastic … the problems are
least on the road in this season."

"The monsoon is quite worse on the Jomsom Muktinath road section … lots of
landslide sections and rock-falling zones."

Recommends 4WD jeep specifically for the Beni–Jomsom and Jomsom–Muktinath
sections.

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
Operator-grade seasonal baseline: April–May–June is the optimal driving
window; monsoon (mid-Jun onward) is the dangerous one. 15 May falls inside
the operator's "fantastic" window.
