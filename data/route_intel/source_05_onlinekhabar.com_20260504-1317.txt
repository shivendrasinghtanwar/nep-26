Title: सडक सीमा निर्धारण नहुँदा बेनीमा कालोपत्रे गर्न ढिलाइ
       (Road-boundary issues delaying blacktopping at Beni)
Date published: 2026-04-28 16:38 (Vaisakh 15, 2083)
Author: Online Khabar / RASS
Fetched: 2026-05-04

KEY EXTRACT:
A 760 m stretch near Waribeni was excluded from the contract because
demolishing roadside structures requires homeowner consent; additional
sections were dropped due to landslide risk. Locals report 10–15 minute
traffic jams on the narrow segment. Project Chief Tejswi Sharma:
"80 national highways lack proper boundary demarcation, making
implementation extremely difficult."

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
The Maldhunga–Beni stretch has minor pinch-points (Waribeni 760 m, Kerabari
100 m, Pharse 100 m) where blacktopping is incomplete and short delays
(10–15 min) are common — annoying, not blocking.
