Title: Landslide Blocks Major Highways And Road Sections
Date published: 2026-05-03 (Sunday)
Author: RSS Nepal (Rastriya Samachar Samiti, Nepal's national news agency)
Fetched: 2026-05-04 (UA: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; NepalTripPrep/1.0))

KEY EXTRACT:
"Various major highways and road sections across the country have been blocked
for traffic due to landslides triggered by pre-monsoon rains for the past few
days."

"The Beni-Jomsom road section in Jalathale, Annapurna Rural Municipality-3,
Myagdi, are operational for one-way traffic only."

Other routes (Araniko Highway and sections in Bagmati, Lamjung, Pyuthan)
remain completely blocked. The piece warns travellers about ongoing
pre-monsoon landslide risk.

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
As of 2026-05-03 the Beni–Jomsom artery is OPEN but restricted to one-way
traffic at the Jalathale (Myagdi) pinch-point — passable, not closed.
