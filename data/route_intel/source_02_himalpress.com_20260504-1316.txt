Title: बेनी–जोमसोम सडकको सिरुबारीमा पहिरो रोक्न 'बेञ्चिङ' विधि
       (Benching Method to Prevent Landslides in Sirubari on Beni-Jomsom Road)
Date published: 2026-03-25 (11 Chaitra 2082)
Author: Himal Press (no individual byline)
Fetched: 2026-05-04

KEY EXTRACT:
"पछिल्लो तीन वर्षयता वर्खायाममा सिरुबारीको सडकमाथिबाट पहिरो खसेर दैनिक जसो
बेनी–जोमसोम सडक अवरुद्ध हुने गरेको छ।"
("For the past three monsoon seasons, landslides at Sirubari have been
disrupting the Beni–Jomsom road almost daily during the rains.")

The Beni–Jomsom–Korala road project has begun the "benching" technique at
Sirubari, Raghugunga Municipality-2, Myagdi — step-cut terraces on the
unstable hillside. Project Chief Tejaswi Sharma said temporary measures
should permit regular traffic through the next monsoon season after the
remedial works.

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
Sirubari (Myagdi) is a known recurring landslide pinch-point on the
Beni–Jomsom road; mitigation works are in progress as of late March 2026,
which corroborates the May-2026 one-way restriction reports.
