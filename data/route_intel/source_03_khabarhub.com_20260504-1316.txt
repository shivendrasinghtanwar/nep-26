Title: Snowfall hits Upper Mustang and Muktinath
Date published: 2026-04-05 12:02
Author: Khabarhub (no individual byline)
Fetched: 2026-05-04

KEY EXTRACT:
"Areas from Syangboche to Korala ... have received up to one foot of snow,"
with officials warning of two more feet possible if conditions persisted
through the evening. The article cites this as the SIXTH significant snowfall
of the season, which began 27 October.

Lower Mustang — including Jomsom — got "heavy rain instead." Pilgrim
inconvenience is reported in Muktinath; no full road closure is specified.

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
Muktinath (3,800 m) area saw snow as recently as 5 April 2026; Jomsom and
the Beni–Jomsom corridor saw rain — relevant for surface conditions but
not road-closing on its own.
