Title: Maldhunga–Beni road upgrade completed
Date published: 2026-02-20
Author: Khabarhub
Fetched: 2026-05-04

KEY EXTRACT:
"The upgrading of the Maldhunga–Beni road, which connects Myagdi and Mustang
to the national highway network, has been completed."

The 13 km stretch (Pokhara-side approach to Beni) was widened to 11 m and
asphalted to a "standard two-lane road." Project Chief Tejashwi Sharma:
"blacktopping and slope stabilization work has been completed on all but
960 m of the road." Travel time on this segment dropped from ~1 hour to
"15 to 20 minutes."

ONE-LINE TAKEAWAY:
The Pokhara-side approach to Beni is now sealed two-lane blacktop with
faster transit — the bad-road portion only starts AFTER Beni.
