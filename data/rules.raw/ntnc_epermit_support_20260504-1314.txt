 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
 What is different counter payment and online payment?
 Online Payment:
 You can pay the permit fee online using Visa or MasterCard. After successful payment, your permit will be sent directly to your email, and you do not need to visit the EP Counter to collect the permit. Please note that an additional 2.9% payment gateway charge will apply for online payments.
 Counter Payment:
 First, you need to fill out the application form online. After submitting the form successfully, you will receive a Permit Request Application. Please present this application at the EP Counter, pay the permit fee in cash, and bring your passport or any valid identification to complete the payment and collect your permit. No additional charge applies for Counter Payment.
 What documents do I need for the permit?
 You need a valid passport (for indian - Aadhar Card / Voter ID), a recent photo (required for online payments only, counter pay no need photo), and complete personal details including name, nationality, date of birth, gender, occupation, and contact information. Children under 10 do not require a permit.
 How long does it take to get the permit?
 Permits are processed immediately after successful payment and form submission. You can print your permit directly from the website once approved. For counter payments, you may need to visit the office to collect it.
 Is the permit refundable?
 No, trekking permits are non-transferable and non-refundable. Please carefully plan your trekking dates, itinerary, and ensure all details are correct before submitting your application to avoid any issues.
 Can I apply for multiple people at once?
 Yes, you can add multiple visitors in a single application. Use the "Add More" button to include additional family members or group participants. Each person's details must be entered separately.
 What is the trekking permit fee?
 The permit fee is NRs. 3,000 for foreigners and NRs. 1,000 for SAARC nationals, including tax.
 Is there any additional charge for online payment?
 Yes. If you choose Online Payment, an additional 2.9% payment gateway charge will be applied.
 Is there any additional charge for counter payment?
 No. If you choose Counter Payment, no additional charge applies, and you can pay the permit fee in cash at the EP Counter.
 Will the permit fee be refunded if I cancel my trip?
 No. Trekking permits are non-refundable and non-transferable.
 What happens if I get my permit at a check-post?
 Double fees will be charged for permits issued directly at check-posts.
 I paid online and the payment was deducted from my account, but I did not receive my permit by email. What should I do?
 If your payment was successful but you did not receive the permit email, please try retrieving your permit from the following link:
 https://epermit.ntnc.org.np/application/print-permit
 Steps:
 1. Select your Area.
 2. Enter your Passport Number and Date of Birth.
 3. Click the “Verify & Retrieve Permit” button.
 Your permit should be available for download.
 If you are still unable to retrieve your permit, please contact us by sending your payment success confirmation to:
 WhatsApp/Viber: +977 9856038358 / +977 9769494100
 Email: epermit@ntnc.org.np
