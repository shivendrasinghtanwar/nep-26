DOTM "अत्यन्त जरुरी सूचना" / Very Urgent Notice — published 2083.01.08 (= 2026-04-21).

Subject: Clarification of inter-province public-transport fare adjustment of 2082.12.25 (= 2026-04-07).
Content: The fare increase that took effect on 2082.12.25 — adjusting passenger and cargo public-vehicle tariffs under the automatic fare-adjustment system following a rise in international fossil-fuel prices — applies ONLY to diesel-engine public vehicles. It does NOT apply to electric public vehicles. Reports have been received that some EV operators were collecting the increased fare without issuing tickets. EV operators must charge the previous (saavik) fare and must compulsorily issue passenger tickets. Violations will be acted on under Motor Vehicles and Transport Management Act 2049 and Regulations 2054.

Relevance to foreign-vehicle/Indian-SUV yatayat fee or 30-day cap: NONE. This notice is exclusively about domestic public-transport tariffs (passenger buses + freight) and does not address foreign-registered vehicles, daily yatayat fees, or the 30-day cumulative cap.
