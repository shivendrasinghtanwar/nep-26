If yes, please get in touch with a Government registered trekking agency to arrange for the trekking guide and TIMS Card.For Trekking Agencies OnlyIn order to buy TIMS Card, agencies shall have to complete a registration form with details of trekkers, guides and trekking routes.Procedure to submit the form:Please click here and register to fill the form. Submit the form with all the mandatory fields.Check out for payment through provided payment services (e.g. ConnectIPS, nepalpay etc.)Once the payment is successful, eCard for TIMS will be generated for each trekker with QR in it.The system is generally available all the time.For charges, NPR 1,000 for SAARC (Citizens of South Asian countries) country applicants and for others NPR 2,000 and, currently payable online.For further information & answers to your specific questions, please click here
 Nepal Tourism Board is a national tourism organization of Nepal established in 1998 by an Act of Parliament in the form of partnership between the Government of Nepal and private sector tourism industry to develop and market Nepal as an attractive tourist destination. The Board provides platform for vision-drawn leadership for Nepal’s tourism sector by integrating Government commitment with the dynamism of private sector.
