About Sunauli Border (India-Nepal) Border Crossing

The Sunauli Border (India-Nepal) is a land border crossing between India and Nepal, located in South Asia. This crossing point connects Gorakhpur with Bhairahawa and is popular with Pilgrims, Backpackers, Cultural Tourists.

Wait Time Expectations

Average crossing time at Sunauli Border (India-Nepal) ranges from 25 to 60 minutes under normal conditions. However, wait times can vary significantly based on time of day, day of week, and season.

Best Time to Cross

To minimize your wait time at Sunauli Border (India-Nepal), consider crossing during these optimal times: 06:00-07:00, 14:00-16:00. Avoid peak hours which are typically 08:00-10:00 and 17:00-19:00.

Crossing Tips

  • Gateway to Lumbini (Buddha's birthplace)
  • Better facilities than other India-Nepal borders
  • Free visa on arrival for most nationalities
  • Currency exchange available

Available Facilities

At the Sunauli Border (India-Nepal) border crossing, you'll find: ATM, Currency Exchange, Hotels, Restaurants, Shops.
