## Buffer Strategy

- Total 15 days actual + 2–3 days of permit buffer = max ~18 days against 30-day cap.
- If a day slips on Mustang side (landslide, weather), borrow from 19 May leisure or 20 May work day.
- Last-resort fallback: skip Lumbini-revisit on 21 May and add to Pokhara → Sunauli buffer.
