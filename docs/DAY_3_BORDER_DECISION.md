# Day 3 — Stop at Bhairahawa or push to Pokhara?
*Compiled 2026-05-05*

## Recommendation

**STOP AT BHAIRAHAWA** — A Pokhara-by-night push collapses on three independent constraints (Sunauli's 9:45 PM vehicle cutoff, the firm-registered SUV Bhansar/Yatayat unknowns that statistically chew 2–4+ hours, and the 2026-02-23 Trishuli-river bus crash on the very night-driving stretch we'd be hitting at 11 PM); the existing Day-3 Bhairahawa halt is the safer plan. **One conditional override below for very-best-case days.**

## Border timing reality

- **Typical clearance, Indian-registered private vehicle:** ~25–60 min crossing under "normal conditions" if all docs are in order, plus the Bhansar (Customs) + Yatayat (Transport) office workflow that runs **10:00 AM–5:00 PM** at the inland counters. Source: [Triploko Sunauli wait-time monitor](https://triploko.com/travel-tools/border-crossing/india-nepal-sunauli) (current 2026 page); [Motologs Bhansar guide — comments thread updated through 2025–2026](https://motologs.com/documents-and-permit-required-for-bhansar-entering-nepal-by-bike-car/) confirming "Offices open 10 AM–5 PM" and that "an authorisation letter, if notarised, is an added benefit" for firm-owned vehicles.
- **Worst case, firm-registered SUV (proprietor ≠ driver):** Realistic 3–4+ hours if the duty officer wants to verify the mom-signed authorisation chain or pulls the vehicle into "secondary" for inspection. The CC playbook 01 verify pass found no 2026 circular addressing firm-registered foreign vehicles where proprietor ≠ driver — this means treatment is at the discretion of the on-duty officer, with notarised letter as the friction-remover. Source: [`claude-code/HANDOFF.md` 2026-05-04 13:35 IST entry](../claude-code/HANDOFF.md) "no 2026 circular found mentioning firm-registered foreign vehicles where proprietor ≠ driver".
- **Queue spikes:** Peak hours **08:00–10:00** and **17:00–19:00** (per Triploko, with **early-morning bus rush from ~04:00** noted on Wikivoyage Sunauli). Optimal windows are **06:00–07:00** and **14:00–16:00**. Indian-side commercial truck waves cluster against the **9:45 PM vehicle cutoff** — Third Eye Traveller's photo essay at the border at 9:30 PM shows "the queue of truckers below who didn't make it and were waiting until morning". Source: [Third Eye Traveller — Sunauli border survival guide](https://thirdeyetraveller.com/india-nepal-sonauli-border/).
- **Firm-registered nuance:** Mom signed the laminated authorisation letter, it is notarised, and Nepal-specific "personal/non-commercial travel" language is on it (per `claude-code/context/firm-paperwork.md`). This is the strongest document-set possible without a 2026 circular. The cap risk is therefore *time*, not *refusal* — but time-risk is what kills the Pokhara-by-night plan.

## Mahendra Highway after dark

Top three hazards (most-recent-first):

1. **Bhaisegauda / Charaundi curve cluster on Prithvi Highway.** A Pokhara→Kathmandu bus lost control at **~01:15 AM on 2026-02-23**, plunged into the Trishuli at Charaundi (Benighat Rorang Rural Municipality, Dhading) — 18 dead, 25 injured. The **Bhairahawa→Pokhara route uses the same Prithvi Highway corridor** from Mugling onward — same curve geometry, opposite direction, same time-of-night profile if you push. Source: [Kathmandu Post — Bus plunges into Trishuli river, 18 killed (2026-02-23)](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2026/02/23/bus-plunges-into-trishuli-river-18-killed). Coverage corroborated by [Nepal Gateway Trekking blog (published 2026-03-08)](https://nepalgatewaytrekking.com/blog/pokhara-to-kathmandu-travel-after-the-accident) advising "avoid night buses, prefer ~7 AM departures … narrow curves, steep hills, and long driving hours" as the named hazards.
2. **Pokhara–Mugling four-laning still under way.** The widening project hit only **43.34% physical progress / 29.47 km blacktopped** by early 2026 (deadline now mid-Jan-2026, repeatedly slipped). Single-lane diversions and unmarked construction zones on a left-hand-drive road are the dark-hours killer. Source: [Ecosphere News — Pokhara–Mugling Road Project review](https://www.ecospherenews.com/detail/571).
3. **Slow truck convoys + overtake friction Mugling→Pokhara.** Single-lane stretches force passing into oncoming traffic; locals advise budgeting **6–7 hours for Bhairahawa→Pokhara even in daylight** (per the existing `docs/ITINERARY.md` Day-4 estimate, supported by Tripadvisor Pokhara forum threads). Adding the border day on top of this is what creates the night-arrival risk.

2026 news mentions consulted: 2026-02-23 Trishuli bus accident, 2026-03-08 follow-up safety guide, ongoing widening reports through Q1 2026.

## Lumbini side-trip math

- **Mayadevi Temple opens 06:00–18:00**, last entry typically 17:30. Sacred Garden + Ashoka Pillar are inside the same ticketed zone, also sunrise-to-sunset. Source: [Holidify Mayadevi Temple page (2026)](https://www.holidify.com/places/lumbini/maya-devi-temple-sightseeing-1257526.html) cross-checked with [Travel Triangle 2026 guide](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/maya-devi-temple/).
- **Useful visit:** Mayadevi inner sanctum + Marker Stone + Pushkarini pond + Ashoka Pillar = **45–75 min**. A full Lumbini Sacred Garden circuit including the international monastic zone needs 2–3 hours and an e-rickshaw.
- **From a Bhairahawa-town hotel** (Tiger Palace / Bhairahawa Garden), Lumbini Sacred Garden is **22–25 km / 35–45 min drive**. From Buddha Maya Garden by KGH (inside Lumbini Sanskritik Zone), it's a 5-min walk to the Mayadevi gate.
- **Inbound (Day 3) vs outbound (Day 13):** Day-3 light visit is realistic only if the border clears by ~13:00 (≈90 min Lumbini detour + return + workday evening). Day-13 (2026-05-21) is already an outbound Pokhara→Bhairahawa drive day with a planned final Lumbini visit — the heavier visit is better there. **Recommend: skip Lumbini on Day 3 unless border clears by 13:00; do the proper visit on Day 13.**

**Operational rule:** all three accept post-22:00 arrivals if you call ahead with an ETA. None will turn you away on a confirmed booking, but a property-level fibre speedtest still must happen at check-in (per `data/hotels.json` red-flag).

## Bhairahawa hotel pick

For a **single-night border-day stop** with a firm-registered Thar Roxx, the priority order is: **secure parking > short drive from Belahiya counter > food after 21:00 > fibre for the evening workday**.

- **Top pick (if budget allows): Tiger Palace by Soaltee, Tilottama.** "Just 8 km north of the India-Nepal (Sunauli) border" per the hotel's own listing, free private valet parking, integrated 5-star resort with on-site casino + 24-hour F&B (eats the "where do I find dinner at 22:00" risk on a slow border day). Free high-speed WiFi. **Caveat:** above the INR 5,500 hotels.json budget ceiling — `data/hotels.json` flags it. Source: [Tripadvisor Tiger Palace 2026 reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g17710100-d12930276-Reviews-Tiger_Palace_By_Soaltee-Tilottama_Lumbini_Zone_Western_Region.html).
- **Best in-budget primary: Buddha Maya Garden by KGH Group, Lumbini Sanskritik Zone.** Free private parking confirmed, 24h front desk, 5-min walk to Mayadevi gate (lets a same-evening 30-min darshan happen if border clears before sunset). Tradeoff: 22 km farther from the Belahiya counter than Tiger Palace, so an 18:00 border exit means arrival ~19:00 vs ~18:30. Source: [Tripadvisor Buddha Maya 2026](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g424940-d15142132-Reviews-Buddha_Maya_Garden_Hotel-Lumbini_Lumbini_Zone_Western_Region.html).
- **Fallback: Bhairahawa Garden Resort.** In-budget, free private parking, 24h reception, simple, fast — best when you only need shower/dinner/sleep before next morning's Pokhara push.

**Recommendation for the 2026-05-11 inbound night: Tiger Palace if budget approved, else Buddha Maya Garden.** Bhairahawa Garden Resort is the right pick for the **2026-05-21 outbound night** (per `data/hotels.json` notes) since you only need quick board+sleep before the Sunauli exit drive.

## Decision matrix

| If border clears by… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Before 13:00** (best case, ~3h crossing) | Light Lumbini darshan (~45 min, Mayadevi only), then Bhairahawa hotel by 16:00. Evening workday. **Stick with Day-3 Bhairahawa halt — safer than pushing 6–7h overnight to Pokhara.** |
| **13:00–15:00** (typical) | Skip Lumbini (save for Day 13). Direct to Bhairahawa hotel. Bhansar + Yatayat receipts photographed, NTC SIM bought. Evening workday. **Confirm Day-3 Bhairahawa halt.** |
| **15:00–17:00** (slow border day) | No Lumbini, no detours. Drive straight to Bhairahawa hotel before dusk. **Day-3 Bhairahawa halt is mandatory — the Pokhara push from here would arrive ~23:00–01:00 on the Trishuli-curve corridor.** |
| **After 17:00 (worst case — verification hold, commercial truck wave, etc.)** | Closest Bhairahawa hotel (Tiger Palace, 8 km), no Lumbini. Get fed, sleep, leave 06:00 next morning fresh. **Day-3 Bhairahawa halt is non-negotiable.** |

**Conditional PUSH** is theoretically defensible only if: (a) you cleared the border before noon, (b) all permits and SIM are in hand by 12:30, (c) you skip Lumbini, (d) you arrive Mugling-Pokhara stretch in daylight (Pokhara by 19:00 latest), and (e) co-driver is fresh. This requires a perfect border day — too many variables to plan around. **Default to STOP.**
