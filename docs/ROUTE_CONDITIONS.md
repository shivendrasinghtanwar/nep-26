# Beni–Jomsom Road — Condition Snapshot
*Compiled 2026-05-04*

## Recommendation

**DRIVE** — As of 2026-05-03 (national wire / RSS Nepal), Beni–Jomsom is open with one-way regulation only at the Jalathale, Myagdi pinch-point; the trip's 2026-05-15 4×4 leg falls inside the April–May–June "best window" called out by every dated source consulted.

## Top three concerns (most-recent first)

1. **Pre-monsoon landslides triggered by recent rain (2026-05-03 wire).** Beni–Jomsom is one-way at Jalathale right now; multiple other Nepal highways are fully blocked for the same reason. Monsoon onset can convert "one-way" to "closed" with little warning. Margin between today and the leg is 11 days.
2. **Sirubari (Raghugunga Mun.-2, Myagdi) chronic landslide zone.** Himal Press (2026-03-25) confirms three consecutive monsoons of near-daily disruption here; mitigation ("benching") is in progress but not done. Expect rolling 1–6 hour holds even on a "normal" day.
3. **Residual snow / runoff in the Muktinath approach.** Sixth snowfall of the 2025-26 season hit Muktinath on 2026-04-05 (Khabarhub) — five weeks pre-leg, but the unusually wet/snowy season means lingering meltwater across switchbacks and expect rockfall on the Jomsom → Muktinath ascent (Day 8, 2026-05-16).

## What to verify on 2026-05-13 in Pokhara

- Walk into the **NTB office** (also where ACAP is bought) and ask the duty officer: current status of the Jalathale one-way and the Sirubari benching works.
- Phone the **Ghasa Police Post** (between Tatopani and Lete) — they're the chokepoint authority for landslides. Ask: any active blocks on 14–15 May?
- Cross-check with **Beni-bound transport entrepreneurs** at Lakeside Pokhara taxi/jeep stands — their morning radio chatter is faster than any news site.
- Look at **Pokhara–Jomsom flight status** (Tara Air / Summit Air) for 13–14 May. Suspended flights = bad weather = bad road.
- Check **Himalaya Times / Khabarhub** front pages for any "highway blocked" stories filed between 2026-05-04 and 2026-05-13.
- If two of the above five say "blocked" — switch to the **HOLD/POSTPONE** alternate plan: skip Mustang, redeploy Days 6–9 to Bandipur (1 night) + Chitwan (2 nights) + extra Pokhara workday.
