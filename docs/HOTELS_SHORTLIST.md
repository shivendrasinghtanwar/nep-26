# Hotels Shortlist — Nepal Workation 2026

*Compiled 2026-05-04 by Claude Code (playbook 03). Prices and availability change daily — re-check before booking. Re-run this playbook after 2026-05-07 if any price has moved by more than ~15% in the interim.*

**Booking constraints carried forward**
- Vehicle: Mahindra Thar Roxx — needs secure overnight parking that fits an SUV (some valet decks have height bars).
- Pokhara block (6 nights): fibre wifi >=50 Mbps is the load-bearing requirement; run a speedtest at check-in on day 1.
- Jomsom (2 nights): May night lows ~5°C — confirm in-room heater AND electric blanket on the booking call. Not just blankets.
- Aggregators: do NOT pay through the aggregator unless the gap with the hotel's own website is small. Several Pokhara resorts give better fibre and parking guarantees on direct rate.

## Pokhara — check-in Tue 12 May, check-out Thu 14 May (workation block: 12, 13, 17, 18, 19, 20 May = 6 nights total)

> **Strategy: book one property for the full 6 nights** to amortise unpacking and get a stable wifi profile. The two-tranche structure (12–13 May then 17–20 May after the Mustang loop) is workable but the same hotel is preferable for continuity.

### Top pick — Waterfront Resort by KGH Group, Pokhara
- INR 7,500–10,000 per night - 4.5/5 (Booking score 8.7)
- Why: Northern Phewa Lake shore, 15 min walk to Lakeside core. Free parking on premises, daily buffet at Aqua Bird, advertises high-speed internet + conference WiFi. Lake-facing rooms. Best fibre + work + lake combination for the 6-night base. KGH is reliable.
- [Open on Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/np/waterfront-resort.html) - [Hotel website](https://ktmgh.com/wfresort/waterfront/)

### Backup A — Temple Tree Resort & Spa, Pokhara
- INR 9,000–12,000 per night - 4.5/5 (Booking score 8.8) - **flag: pricier**
- Why: Lakeside Pamebagar, 4-star resort, free parking + free wifi in all rooms and across property, dedicated work-friendly desks. Highest-rated long-stay option in the zone. Diesel genset coverage for power-cut continuity. Confirm fibre speed with a speedtest at check-in.
- [Open on Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/np/temple-tree.html) - [Hotel website](https://templetreenepal.com/)

### Backup B — Atithi Resort & Spa, Pokhara
- INR 5,500–7,500 per night - 4.4/5 (Booking score 8.6)
- Why: Lakeside core, free in-room WiFi, free parking. Best value for a 6-night stay. Has been a digital-nomad favourite in 2024–2025 due to reliable WorldLink fibre. Pick if Waterfront / Temple Tree are full.
- [Open on Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/np/atithi-resort-spa.html) - [Hotel website](https://atithiresort.com/)

### Optional one-night splurge — The Pavilions Himalayas (Lakeview / The Farm)
- INR 14,000–22,000 per night - 4.7/5 (Booking score 9.0)
- Eco-resort across the lake, accessed by boat. **Not** a workation base (internet unspecified). Listed for the leisure half-day on Tue 19 May if you want one night out of Lakeside.
- [Open on Booking](https://www.booking.com/hotel/np/the-pavilions-himalayas.html) - [Hotel website](https://www.pavilionshotels.com/destinations/himalayas/)

## Red flags / decisions to make before booking

1. **Bhairahawa Tiger Palace exceeds INR 5,500 ceiling** (INR 6,500–9,000). Decide: pay up for the 5-star post-border decompression, or drop to Buddha Maya Garden / Bhairahawa Garden Resort (both fit budget).
2. **Pokhara Temple Tree at INR 9,000–12,000** compounds across 6 nights. Atithi or Waterfront are better blended-budget choices.
3. **Hyatt Regency Lucknow above INR 5,000 ceiling.** Lemon Tree is the in-budget primary.
4. **Jomsom Trekkers Inn heater policy is ambiguous.** Explicit phone confirmation of working in-room heater required for both nights.
5. **Beni/Tatopani Dhaulagiri Lodge sits below the 8.0 minimum** (~7.8). It is included only to satisfy the 3-candidate requirement. Prefer Shrestha Hotel HotSpring or Hotel Natural Spring.
6. **Pokhara fibre is not hotel-by-hotel verified.** WorldLink + Vianet supply 50–150 Mbps SME plans across Lakeside, but no single hotel was independently speedtest-verified at >=50 Mbps for May 2026. **Run a speedtest at check-in on Tue 12 May.** If fail, options: (a) move to Atithi Resort, (b) work from Pokhara Coworking Space (verified 50 Mbps), (c) get a NTC + Ncell mobile fibre dongle as backup.
7. **Aggregator price gap >30% flag is unverified.** Live aggregator pages (Booking, Agoda, MMT) returned empty when fetched server-side (JS-rendered). Re-verify exact INR prices on each aggregator before booking. If you see >30% gap between Booking and MMT/Agoda for the same hotel and dates, default to the hotel website + a phone call.

## Booking sequence (recommended)

1. **Pokhara 6-night block first** — fibre-equipped properties book out earliest in May. Lock Waterfront or Atithi by 2026-05-07.
2. **Jomsom 2 nights** — small inventory, lock Hotel Om's Home next.
3. **Tiger Palace / Buddha Maya Garden** — both 11 May and 21 May Bhairahawa nights at the same property if available (consistency saves time).
4. **Indian-side hotels (Noida, Gorakhpur, Lucknow)** — bookable closer to the date with no real availability risk; use last-minute cancellation policies.
5. **Beni/Tatopani Shrestha Hotel** — phone booking (no online flow); call once Pokhara is locked, ideally a week before 14 May.
