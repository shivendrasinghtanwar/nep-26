# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Bhansar** | Nepal Customs duty / permit. For Indian vehicles, a daily-fee permit issued at the border by Nepal Department of Customs. Required. |
| **Yatayat** | "Transport" in Nepali. Refers to the daily-fee transport permit issued at the border by the Nepal Department of Transport Management. Distinct from Bhansar. Both are required. |
| **e-RC** | Electronic Registration Certificate for an Indian vehicle. A printed colour copy with self-attestation (and firm stamp where applicable) is what Nepal customs accepts. |
| **PUC** | Pollution Under Control certificate. Indian requirement; Nepal sometimes asks for sight of it at the border. |
| **30-day cumulative cap** | 2026 rule restricting any single Indian private vehicle to a maximum of 30 cumulative days inside Nepal per calendar year. Overstay penalty: vehicle impoundment. |
| **Sunauli** | The most-used India–Nepal land border crossing for travellers headed to Pokhara. Sits opposite Bhairahawa on the Nepal side. |
| **Bhairahawa** | First major Nepal town past Sunauli. Also the launchpad for Lumbini. Has decent hotels, fuel, and SIM kiosks. |
| **Mahendra Highway** | The east-west arterial highway across Nepal's Terai (lowland). Bhairahawa → Butwal → Mugling → spur to Pokhara. |
| **Mustang** | Trans-Himalayan region north of Pokhara. Two zones: **Lower Mustang** (Jomsom, Kagbeni, Muktinath — open to ACAP holders) and **Upper Mustang** (Lo Manthang area — restricted, USD 500/10 days). |
| **ACAP** | Annapurna Conservation Area Permit, issued by NTNC. Required to enter Mustang beyond Beni. SAARC nationals (Indians) NPR 1,000 per person; foreigners NPR 3,000. |
| **TIMS** | Trekkers' Information Management System card. Required only for trekking, not for road travel. Issued at NTB (Pokhara / Kathmandu) or via TAAN. |
| **Beni** | Last reliable fuel + ATM town before the Mustang gravel begins. Mid-altitude (~830 m). |
| **Tatopani** | Hot-spring town between Beni and Ghasa. Common acclimatisation halt. |
| **Jomsom** | District HQ of Mustang. ~2,700 m altitude. The recommended overnight halt before Muktinath darshan. Has a small airport, fuel, and Nepal Police presence. |
| **Kagbeni** | Village just before the final climb to Muktinath. Sometimes serves as the boundary marker between Lower and Upper Mustang — re-confirm classification each year. |
| **Muktinath** | Hindu / Buddhist pilgrimage site at ~3,800 m. The 108-spout bath and the eternal flame are the main reasons people make the climb. |
| **NTB** | Nepal Tourism Board. Has an office in Pokhara that issues ACAP permits on the spot with 2 photos + ID. |
| **NTNC** | National Trust for Nature Conservation — manages ACAP. Source of the official ACAP fee table. |
| **NTC / Ncell** | The two Nepali mobile operators. NTC has better Mustang coverage; Ncell is faster in Pokhara. |
| **eSewa / Khalti** | Nepali UPI-equivalents. Worth setting up if you want to pay by QR in cities. |
| **CIWEC** | Travel-medicine specialist hospital chain in Kathmandu and Pokhara. Go-to for altitude or diarrhoea cases. |
| **Diamox** | Acetazolamide. Acute mountain sickness prophylaxis. Used on the Muktinath leg. Prescription drug. |
| **Thar Roxx** | The five-door Mahindra Thar (2024 onward). Has a part-time 4×4 system with 4-Low. The trip's vehicle. |
| **Thar Digital Services (TDS)** | Shive's sole-proprietorship firm, registered owner of the Thar Roxx. The authorization letter for personal travel uses TDS letterhead. |
