Route & Driving

Three terrains, three driving modes — the expressway chain, the Mahendra Highway, the Mustang 4×4.

Phase 1 — The Expressway Chain (India)

Day 1

Bikaner → Noida — ~610 km, 9–10 h

NH11 to Sikar/Behror, then Western Peripheral Expressway around Delhi to Noida. Heavy on tolls. Plan two long fuel stops, no more.

Day 2

Noida → Gorakhpur — ~720 km, 10–11 h

Yamuna Expressway → Agra–Lucknow Expressway → Purvanchal Expressway → Gorakhpur Link. Almost continuous expressway. Aim Gorakhpur by 7 PM.

Phase 2 — Border & Mahendra Highway (Nepal)

Day 3

Gorakhpur → Sunauli → Bhairahawa — ~120 km

Reach Sunauli around mid-morning. Process: Indian customs exit → walk-distance to Nepal side → Bhansar (customs) → Yatayat (transport) → tourist SIM → exchange counter. Allow 3 hours minimum at the border.

Day 4

Bhairahawa → Pokhara — ~190 km, 6–7 h

Mahendra Highway via Butwal → Mugling → Pokhara. Smooth tar, scenic. Buy ACAP next day at the NTB office in Pokhara.

Phase 3 — The Mustang 4×4 leg

Day 6 — Acclimatise

Pokhara → Beni / Tatopani — ~85 km

Tar road via Naya Pul. Tatopani hot springs are the reward. Sleep at ~1,200 m to start the altitude curve.

Day 7 — 4WD

Beni → Jomsom — ~75 km, 5–6 h

The road becomes gravel, narrow, with multiple river crossings. Engage 4WD-Low. Slow pace through Tatopani → Ghasa → Lete → Tukuche → Marpha → Jomsom (~2,700 m). Acclimatise overnight.

Day 8 — Darshan

Jomsom → Muktinath → Jomsom — ~40 km round

Pre-dawn start. Climb past Kagbeni to Muktinath (~3,800 m). Allow 90 minutes for darshan + 108-spout bath. Back to Jomsom for lunch.

Day 9 — Descend

Jomsom → Pokhara — ~160 km, 7–8 h

Reverse the gravel descent. Easy pace. Pokhara recovery night with fibre + lakeside dinner.

Fuel strategy

India leg

Tank-up at every state-border pump — toll plazas have HP/IOC nearby. Final India fill at Gorakhpur before border.

Nepal lowland

Bhairahawa, Butwal, Mugling, Pokhara — all have multi-pump stations. Quality is fine for Thar Roxx.

Mustang

Reliable pumps only at Beni and Jomsom. Top up at both. The 10 L jerrycan is your insurance — fill it at Beni, never carry full across the border.

Where to slow down

Mugling–Pokhara curves

Frequent overtaking accidents. Stay behind buses; don't overtake into blind curves.

Tatopani–Ghasa landslide zones

This stretch is geologically active. Fresh debris is common. Don't stop under loose slopes; move quickly past flagged zones.

Kagbeni → Muktinath switchbacks

Hairpins at altitude. Engine power drops 25–30%. Use 2nd gear; don't lug.

Sunauli border vicinity

Mixed traffic, no lane discipline, slow trucks. Crawl through the bazaar; respect the traffic constable.
